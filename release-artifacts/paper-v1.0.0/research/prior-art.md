# Prior-art register

## Search boundary

This is a preliminary, reproducible search record for the protocol paper, not an exhaustive systematic review. It is limited to primary sources directly bearing on probabilistic scoring, forecast combination, multi-agent debate, and sycophancy. The manuscript makes no novelty claim. A reviewer must assess search coverage and whether the paper contributes beyond composing known mechanisms.
