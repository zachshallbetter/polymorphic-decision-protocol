# Construct stress register

| Stressor | Failure mode | Current evidence | Status | Required response |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shared model provenance is not observable from provider labels alone | ACS treats an assumed independence weight as measured dependence | The specification proposes vendor and evidence-class axes; no validated estimator is supplied | Open | Operationalize dependence and compare it with observed residual dependence before interpreting ACS quantitatively |
| Pressure prompt changes wording or social context | Flip category confounds pressure, new evidence, and ordinary stochastic variation | No admissible multi-vendor corpus is included | Open | Use randomized controlled prompts, repeated trials, frozen context, and blinded coding |
| Human weights are frozen but poorly chosen | Procedural integrity preserves a misspecified value model | Protocol rule only; no outcome study | Open | Separate governance compliance from decision quality; analyze sensitivity and elicit weights transparently |
| One settled case is treated as validation | Outcome bias and selective reporting | Private author-reported case is not independently inspectable here | Blocked | Keep as illustration only; require prospective, complete case registration |
| Brier score comparison has small or dependent samples | Unstable estimates and misleading superiority claims | Test 002 remains unexecuted; no sample-size rationale for general effectiveness | Open | Prespecify estimand, sampling, dependence handling, uncertainty intervals, and minimum information target |
| Decision domains have different stakes and outcome horizons | A single workflow may not transfer | No cross-domain evidence | Open | Restrict claims by domain until portability studies exist |
